Objection register · opened on day 1, public throughout

Sixteen objections, each answered with a number.

What people say about data centers in France and elsewhere, mapped to this site. Each row has an owner, a design answer, a number and a source. Numbers marked derived are our arithmetic and stay labelled until a specialist confirms them. From week 1, residents add rows.

16 of 16
1

It will be loud at night

Nearest neighbors

The station in the corner of its lot furthest from homes on both sides, 520 m from the nearest house; an enclosed engine hall; oversized low-speed dry coolers; barriers; generator tests in daytime only; an independent meter at le Bois Noir, the nearest façade, published live.

At night the campus may add at most 3 dB(A) to the background measured at the nearest façade before construction, and its own sound never exceeds 40 dB(A), whichever is stricter (derived target; acoustician confirms before the permit). The night background is measured at the façade before construction; after opening the acoustician measures the campus's contribution with everything running (residual-level method, NF S31-010) and publishes it every three months. Stricter than the legal night emergence of 4 dB(A) (arrêté du 23 janvier 1997, for a background of 35 to 45 dB(A)). 40 dB(A) is the WHO outdoor night guideline, used here as a ceiling for the campus alone. The campus is designed to 40 dB(A) at the nearest façade, le Bois Noir, 400 m from the nearest building and 530 m from the station (derived).

Arrêté du 23 janvier 1997 (ICPE noise limits), summarised by Venathec; Olds NIA 2026: 72 dB(A) unmitigated at 290 m, 51 after controls, which scales to about 48 dB(A) at 400 m and 46 dB(A) at 520 m. So the campus has to be about 8 dB(A) quieter than the Olds controls at the nearest façade (derived). The enclosed engine hall, silencers on the stacks and the oversized low-speed coolers have to deliver that; the acoustician confirms it before the permit.

2

It will drink our water in a drought

Wider residentsOpponents and press

Closed-loop dry cooling and air-cooled condensers; rainwater harvested; the figure on a public meter.

Under 10,000 m³ a year, none for cooling (derived from CyrusOne Wissous, 850 m³ a year at 60 MW, and Telehouse Marseille, 250 m³). The comparables scale to 1,000 to 1,500 m³ for 100 MW; 10,000 m³ is the commitment ceiling, leaving room for washrooms, cleaning and construction-phase use. An evaporative design would use about 1.9 million m³ a year (derived: 1.8 litres per kWh of facility load, 120 MW, 8,760 hours).

CyrusOne Wissous ICPE file; Préfecture d'Ille-et-Vilaine drought order, June 2026.

3

It will take the region's electricity

Wider residentsMayors and authoritiesOpponents and press

A 180 to 200 MW heat-and-power station on site with one spare unit, plus a 100 MW battery; a 90 kV connection used only to export, with zero import committed by the operating company, verified by RTE's meter monthly, any import published with its cause.

0 MW imported; 0 to 80 MW exported depending on station availability, to a region that produced 31% of its own electricity in 2024.

Insee Conjoncture Bretagne n° 54, Bilan économique 2024, June 2025.

4

A gas plant next to homes, running all the time, is dirty

Opponents and pressParents and schools

Say the number. The station's heat displaces gas boilers through the heat network; a published fuel trajectory; an annual emissions report; low-NOx burners and stacks of about 25 m.

About 530,000 t CO₂ a year running the campus alone; 800,000 to 890,000 t with full export (derived: 2.3 to 3.9 TWh of gas a year at 45% electrical efficiency and 0.227 t CO₂e per MWh, ADEME). Biomethane 20% of the fuel at opening, 50% by 2035 (target, to be costed): about 40% less carbon (derived).

Landivisiau precedent (Power Technology).

6

This is the end of the car plant

Plant workers and unions

The campus takes only three empty lots west of the factory; no production buildings or production space are taken; the factory buys heat from the campus; the factory's own future projects are untouched.

Plant at about 50% capacity, 322 cars a day, 19 stoppage days in 2026, none of which the campus changes.

Unidivers, 5 October 2026, "Stellantis, une usine modernisée qui tourne encore à moitié": 322 cars a day since mid-July, twelve stoppage days before October plus seven from 22 October.

7

What do we get back?

Wider residentsMayors and authorities

Heat at a tiered price; a local benefit fund of at least €1M a year run by a board where residents and the communes hold six of eight seats; a heat co-op residents and the communes own; a 3.7 ha public park; local tax published annually; the academy. To "the local benefit fund is a bribe": it is paid whether or not anyone supports the project, residents and the communes decide what it funds, the campus has no veto, and the accounts are published.

Phase 1: 50 GWh a year. Phase 2: up to 300 GWh a year, about 20,000 gas-heated homes' worth (derived: 300 GWh ÷ 14 MWh), every home offered a connection, the three communes first. €750, €450 or €150 a year saved per gas-heated home by tier, at least 10% below the gas bill everywhere on the network (derived). About €8 to 10M a year in local taxes (derived, to confirm). At least €1M a year into the local benefit fund, plus €5M at financial close; 25 to 49% of the network company for the co-op (subject to the Rennes Métropole concession).

ENGIE Solutions; AMORCE 2024; Effy 2025; Fiscallia.

It is foreign-owned; whose data is it?

Wider residentsOpponents and pressMayors and authorities

A French operating company under EU data law; the sovereign wealth fund named; no non-EU access to tenant data (subject to tenant contracts). Local ownership of the heat network through the co-op. To "the co-op is a risk to my savings": the campus carries the price risk through the transfer price, so a member can lose at most the €50 share.

16

It eats the land we wanted for real industry

Mayors and authoritiesPlant workers and unions

Three empty lots, 16 ha: everything built on one 5.5 ha plot, 3.7 ha given back as a public park, the factory untouched; brownfield reuse under zero net land take. Say it plainly: the built plot, AC 0705, is in the public ZAC La Janais, a zone Rennes Métropole created for business activity, and is bought from its developer. Rennes Métropole decides whether a data center is the activity it wants there.

5.5 ha built on, of the site's roughly 146 ha; the ZAC La Janais covers 52 ha.

Cadastre; Rennes Métropole open data, zones d'aménagement concerté (ZAC La Janais, created 5 April 2018, concession, activities); Géoportail de l'urbanisme (AC 0705 zoned UO4); KizerGrid (no public link; cited from the research notes).

Tax, in brief

Research note behind row 7. To be confirmed by a tax adviser.

  • No data-center-specific IFER exists in France. Local tax arrives through taxe foncière (industrial method: 4% of building cost, 6% of land, one-third abatement; specialised equipment exempt under art. 1382-11° CGI) and CFE on the same rental value, less 30% for industrial premises. CVAE is being phased out.
  • Capital cost (derived): buildings €8 to 10M per MW × 100 MW = €0.8 to 1.0B; the power station about €1M per MW × 200 MW = €0.2B; the battery €300 per kWh × 300,000 kWh = €0.09B. About €1.1 to 1.3B in all.
  • Rough estimate (derived): only buildings and fixed works are taxable, servers and machinery being exempt: about half of the building cost plus the station's civil works, €600 to 700M. Rental value at 4% of that (the industrial method applies 4% to buildings and 6% to land): €24 to 28M. Property tax of €4M to €5.5M and CFE of €4M to €4.5M: around €8 to 10M a year, mostly to Rennes Métropole.
  • A Senate bill on data centers was adopted on 25 March 2026 without its proposed charges, after a committee rejection in February; it is waiting at the Assemblée nationale.
  • Commitment: publish the actual local tax bill every year.

Property values, in brief

Research note behind row 13.

  • Princeton thesis, April 2026. Stacked difference-in-differences on Virginia and Texas: properties within 1.5 miles of a facility against 5 to 10 miles away. The pooled price effect is small and statistically indistinguishable from zero; large facilities show negative effects, small ones none.
  • NAR Data Center Impact Report, September 2026. County-level correlation only; no single pattern across markets.
  • No French study exists.
  • Commitment: annual anonymised sales data within 1 km, and an independent valuation, paid by the campus, for any charter-zone household that puts its home up for sale.
